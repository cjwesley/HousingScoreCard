The Referendum Record

Minority rights on the American ballot, 1865–2020: Black suffrage, women's suffrage, immigration and national origin, religious liberty, school integration, gay and lesbian civil rights, marriage, and housing. 94 entries. Across 150 years, no minority right in this table was created by majority vote; the ballot's documented range runs from stripping rights to ratifying them after courts, legislatures, or constitutional amendments had already moved. Reading the result column. Rights denied: an expansion of rights was rejected by the electorate. Rights stripped: an existing right, ordinance, or approved project was repealed or extinguished by the electorate. Barrier enacted: voters created a special procedural obstacle applying to the minority-rights measure. Rights affirmed: the pro-rights side prevailed. Referendum refused: the governing body declined to submit the question to a popular vote.
